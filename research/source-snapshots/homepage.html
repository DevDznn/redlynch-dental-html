    The very best dental treatment, at competitive prices

    Redlynch Dental is your modern cairns dental practice, located within the Redlynch Commercial Hub. We proudly service the local community of Redlynch, as well as Brinsmead, Edge Hill, Smithfield and the surrounding suburbs of Cairns. We cater to all of your families’ dental needs, from children’s dentistry all the way up to high end cosmetic cases, and everything in between.

    We take great pride in providing you with the very best dental treatment, at competitive prices, in the most relaxing environment possible. Let us know what music we can play for you, or choose a movie to watch whilst you have your treatment completed. We help many anxious patients overcome their apprehensions and complete their dental treatment.

    All of our staff are highly knowledgeable and extremely passionate about the work that we do, it's definitely more than just a job to us! Many of our patients say they wish they had come to see us earlier! We take the time to listen to you and understand what it is that you want to achieve, and then help guide you down the right path to arrive at this destination.

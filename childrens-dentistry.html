Children’s dentistry

Your child’s early experiences at the dentist shape how they feel about dental care for life. We keep visits calm, positive and fun.

Book online (opens the practice's online booking system in a new tab) (07) 4039 4720
From the Redlynch Dental team

Your child’s early experiences at the dentist are so important in shaping their future attitude towards their dental health. Fun, positive early experiences are the best way to make sure your child doesn’t develop unnecessary long-term dental fears.

Early diagnosis and treatment of any problems with your child’s teeth can help reduce the time and money needed to fix them, and prevent unnecessary discomfort.

Free kids dentistry. Redlynch Dental offers free kids dentistry. Eligibility and what is included can vary, so please ask our team about your child’s situation when you book.

Making the first visits easy

Start early

Bringing children in early, before there is a problem, means their first experiences of the dental chair are relaxed and positive.

Gentle, patient care

Dr Julian Fung has a particular passion for working with children and for easing dental fears in patients of all ages.

Distraction helps

Let us know what music your child likes, or choose a movie to watch during their appointment.

Parents are part of it

You’re welcome to stay with your child, ask questions and take home practical tips for brushing at home.

Looking after little teeth at home

A few simple habits make a big difference:

  • Clean your baby’s teeth as soon as they appear, using a soft toothbrush. Under 18 months, only use toothpaste if your child’s dentist recommends it.
  • From 18 months to 5 years, brush twice a day with a soft toothbrush and a small pea-sized amount of fluoride toothpaste. Your dentist may recommend a different toothpaste if your child is at high risk of decay.
  • Children should spit out toothpaste after brushing, rather than swallowing it or rinsing.
  • Help with brushing until your child can do it well on their own. By about 9 years of age, most children can brush and floss their own teeth.
  • Offer water between meals, limit sweet snacks and drinks, and avoid putting a baby to bed with a bottle of milk or juice.

Baby teeth matter. They help children chew, speak and smile with confidence, and they hold space for the adult teeth that follow.

Questions

Children’s Dentistry: your questions answered

Still unsure? Call (07) 4039 4720 and talk to our team.

When should my child first see a dentist?

Children should see a dentist by the time their first tooth appears, or by their first birthday. Early visits help problems be spotted early and make the dental chair familiar. Call us to book.

Is children’s dentistry free?

Redlynch Dental offers free kids dentistry. Please ask our team about eligibility and what is included when you book, so you know what to expect before the visit.

What if my child is nervous?

That’s common. Tell us when you book. We take things slowly, explain each step in child-friendly language, and can play music or a movie to help them relax.

Do baby teeth really need fillings?

Decay in baby teeth can cause pain and infection, and can affect the adult teeth developing underneath. Your dentist will explain the options for your child’s situation.

Our services

Complete dental care for every age and every smile.

From check-ups and children’s dentistry to implants, Invisalign® and emergency care. Choose a service to learn more, or call us and we will point you in the right direction.

Everyday and family care 4 services

The routine care that keeps teeth healthy for life, for every age.

  • Check-ups and Dental Cleans

    A thorough examination, X-rays where needed, a professional scale and clean, and a printed treatment plan.

  • Children’s Dentistry

    Positive early experiences, gentle care and early detection, so children grow up comfortable at the dentist.

  • Fillings

    Treating decay early with tooth-coloured (white) fillings, before it reaches the nerve.

  • Custom Mouthguards

    Custom-fitted sports mouthguards to help protect teeth and jaws during contact sport, for all ages.

Urgent care 1 service

Appointments are reserved every day for dental emergencies.

  • Emergency Dental Care

    Appointments reserved daily for pain, broken teeth, swelling, trauma and broken dentures. Call us straight away.

Restoring and replacing teeth 5 services

Repairing damaged teeth and replacing missing ones, so you can eat, speak and smile comfortably.

  • Crowns and Bridges

    Custom caps that strengthen broken or heavily filled teeth, and fixed bridges that replace missing ones.

  • Dental Implants

    A fixed, natural-looking way to replace one or more missing teeth, planned with 3D imaging.

  • Full Arch Dental Implants

    A fixed set of teeth supported by implants, for people with loose dentures or failing teeth.

  • Dentures

    New full or partial dentures, replacements for loose dentures, and implant-anchored options.

  • Root Canal Therapy

    Saving a tooth whose nerve is inflamed beyond repair or has died, so you can avoid an extraction.

Oral surgery 2 services

Gentle tooth removal, including wisdom teeth, with time to talk through your concerns.

  • Tooth Extractions

    Gentle tooth removal, with time to talk through your concerns and steps to help you relax.

  • Wisdom Teeth

    Assessment with X-rays, conservative care where possible, and in-chair removal when needed.

Cosmetic and smile treatments 5 services

Brightening, straightening and reshaping, planned around your face and your goals.

  • Cosmetic Dentistry

    Whitening, bonding, veneers and Invisalign® explained, with costs in writing before you decide.

  • Cosmetic Veneers

    Thin, custom-made porcelain shells bonded to the front of teeth to change their shape, colour or alignment.

  • Facial Injectables and Skin Treatments

    Dento-facial aesthetic treatments that consider how your face frames your smile. By consultation only.

  • Invisalign® Clear Aligners

    Clear, removable aligners that straighten teeth gradually. Assessed by an Invisalign®-trained dentist.

  • Teeth Whitening

    Professional whitening using the Philips Zoom system, supervised by your dental team.

Treatment suitability depends on an individual clinical assessment. Any surgical or invasive procedure carries risks. Before proceeding, you should seek a second opinion from an appropriately qualified health practitioner.

Ready when you are.

New patients of all ages are welcome. Call our friendly Redlynch team or book online, and we will find a time that suits you.

    Dr. Alex Bornsztejn – Principal Dentist

    Bachelor Health Science in Dentistry, Masters in Dentistry – BHSc(Dent), MDent

    Since early childhood, Alex always wanted to be a Dentist. Alex grew up in Victoria, where he successfully achieved his dental degree from Latrobe University. Alex completed his Masters degree in rural Victoria, before relocating to Queensland. Alex has worked all over Queensland, from Brisbane to Townsville, and now calls Cairns home.

    Alex is passionate about all aspects of general dentistry. He places a high importance on continued education, and regularly attends courses run by leaders in the dental field. His genuine obsession with dentistry has taken him all over the world to learn the very best and latest technology and techniques in regards to dental implants and wisdom teeth surgery, which Alex is excited to share with his patients.

    Outside of dentistry, Alex loves spending time on the water fishing and exploring the outdoors.

    Areas of expertise:
    • General dentistry
    • Implant dentistry
    • Wisdom teeth removal
    • Crowns, veneers & smile makeovers

    Dr. Nadia Mullins – Dentist

    Bachelor of Dental Surgery – BDS

    Originally from Toronto, Canada, Dr. Nadia brings a warm and personable approach to dentistry, paired with a strong foundation in clinical expertise. She completed her Bachelor of Dental Surgery locally at James Cook University in Cairns, graduating in 2018. Following her studies, Dr. Nadia returned home to Canada to practise dentistry closer to family. Over the years, she built a strong reputation for her gentle approach, attention to detail, and commitment to delivering high-quality care. After five years working and living in Toronto, Dr. Nadia is thrilled to call Cairns home once again, drawn back by the lifestyle and community.

    Dr. Nadia has a special interest in cosmetic dentistry and is known for her approachable personality, her ability to explain treatments clearly, and her dedication to making every visit as comfortable and reassuring as possible.

    Outside the dental clinic, Dr. Nadia enjoys staying active and soaking up the outdoors. You’ll often find her running or hiking local trails, exploring new food spots around Cairns, or spending quality time with her husband and their dog.

    Dr. Julian Fung – Dentist

    Bachelor of Dental Surgery – BDS

    Julian is proud to be a FNQ local, completing his studies at JCU in Cairns.
    Julian prides himself on being easy going and extremely gentle with his patients. He has a passion for working with children and eliminating dental fears in all ages, making his patients feel at ease is something he prides himself on. Besides his pleasant and friendly nature, Julian is incredibly talented at many technical aspects of dentistry, and we are grateful to have him as part of our team!

    Outside of work, Julian enjoys spending time with his friends and family, as well as his two sausage dogs Basil and Frankfurt.

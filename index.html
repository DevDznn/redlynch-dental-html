Questions

Things patients often ask us

Can’t see your question? Call us on (07) 4039 4720, or read the full FAQ.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes. Redlynch Dental welcomes new patients of all ages. If it is your first visit, we recommend starting with a thorough consultation and dental clean. What to expect as a new patient.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us straight away on (07) 4039 4720. We reserve specific appointments every day for dental emergencies and will always try to see you as soon as possible. Emergency dental care.

Can I book online?

Yes. You can book online through the practice’s booking system, or call (07) 4039 4720 and our team will find a time for you.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, payment plans are available. Ask our team about the options when you book or at your consultation.

Can I get an appointment outside normal hours?

Our regular hours are Monday to Thursday 8am to 5pm and Friday 8am to 4:30pm. After-hours and weekend appointments are available at request, so call us to ask.

I’m nervous about visiting the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. The team helps many anxious patients overcome their worries and complete their treatment. Let us know what music you would like, or choose a movie to watch during your treatment, and tell us about any concerns when you book.

New patients

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Redlynch Dental welcomes new patients of all ages.

What happens at my first visit?

We recommend starting with a thorough consultation and dental clean. We examine your teeth, gums and jaw joints, take the X-rays you need, photograph any potential problems, scale, clean and polish your teeth, apply fluoride, and give you a printed treatment plan. More about your first visit.

Is there a new patient offer?

We offer new patients a comprehensive check-up, necessary X-rays, a scale, clean and polish, fluoride and a treatment plan: gap free with any health fund, or $199 if you are not in a health fund. Terms and conditions apply. See the offer.

What should I bring?

It helps to bring a list of your medications and any allergies, details of any medical conditions, your health fund card if you have extras cover, and any recent dental X-rays or records if you have them.

General dental care

How often should I have a check-up?

Your dentist will recommend an interval based on your oral health. Many people come in every six to twelve months.

How should I look after my teeth at home?

Brush twice a day with a fluoride toothpaste, clean between your teeth every day, limit sugary foods and drinks, drink tap water, and keep up regular check-ups.

Why are X-rays needed?

X-rays show areas the eye can’t see, such as decay between teeth, under fillings or below the gum line, and the position of wisdom teeth. Your dentist will only recommend X-rays you need.

My gums bleed when I brush. Should I worry?

Bleeding gums can be an early sign of gum inflammation. It is worth booking a check-up and clean. See check-ups and cleans.

Children’s dentistry

When should my child first see a dentist?

Children should see a dentist by the time their first tooth appears, or by their first birthday. Call us to book.

Is children’s dentistry free?

Redlynch Dental offers free kids dentistry. Please ask our team about eligibility and what is included when you book.

What toothpaste should my child use?

A small pea-sized amount of fluoride toothpaste, twice a day, from 18 months to 5 years. Under 18 months, only use toothpaste if your child’s dentist recommends it. Ask us if you’re unsure.

Does my child need a mouthguard?

Children who play contact sport should wear a mouthguard. A mouthguard custom-made by a dentist gives the most protection. See mouthguards.

Emergency dentistry

What counts as a dental emergency?

Painful teeth or gums, broken teeth or fillings, facial swelling, trauma to the face, teeth or gums, and broken or lost dentures. Emergency dental care.

Can I be seen the same day?

Specific appointments are reserved every day for emergencies, and the team will always strive to see you as soon as possible. Call as early as you can.

When should I call 000 instead?

If swelling affects your breathing, swallowing or eye, bleeding won’t stop, you have a serious head or facial injury, or you feel very unwell, call 000 or go to your nearest emergency department.

Cosmetic and restorative treatments

Should I choose whitening or veneers?

Whitening brightens your natural teeth but won’t change fillings, crowns or the shape of teeth. Veneers change shape and colour but involve more treatment. Your dentist can help you decide. See whitening and veneers.

Am I suitable for dental implants?

It depends on your bone, gum health and general health. The practice offers a complimentary implant consultation, with a 3D X-ray, to assess suitability. Dental implants.

How long does Invisalign® take?

It depends on your needs. Some minor cases take as little as seven weeks, but average treatment is around 12 to 18 months. Invisalign®.

Does root canal treatment hurt?

Most root canal treatment is carried out comfortably under local anaesthetic. The tooth may be tender for a few days afterwards. Root canal therapy.

Payment and costs

How much will my treatment cost?

It depends on what you need. After a comprehensive check-up you receive a printed treatment plan with the most accurate estimate we can give of the time and cost involved.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, payment plans are available. Ask our team about the options.

Can I use my health fund?

What you can claim depends on your fund and level of extras cover. Ask your fund what you are covered for, and ask our team when you book. The current new patient offer is advertised as gap free with any health fund.

Is Medicare available for dental care?

Medicare does not cover most general dental care for adults. Some government dental programs exist for eligible people. Check eligibility with Services Australia or Queensland Health, and ask our team whether a particular program can be used at the practice.
